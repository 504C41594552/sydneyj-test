About

Birmingham-born, London-based artist Sydney Jo Jackson crafts a sound that is fearless and addictive. Inspired by legends like Millie Jackson, Tina Turner, and Prince, Sydney fuses old-school soul, blues, and funk with a modern edge.

With over a million streams to date and a #1 spot on Australia's ARIA Dance Chart alongside Husky on "Get Down," Sydney's gritty, soulful voice cuts straight to the heart.
